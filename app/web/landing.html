Questions

Is this legal?

We only fetch pages a shop has made public, we identify ourselves honestly in the user agent, we respect robots.txt, and we rate-limit ourselves to about one request per second per shop. We never log in, never bypass anything, and never touch a checkout.

How fresh are the numbers?

Crawls run weekly on the free plan, daily on Pro, and every twelve hours on Business. You can also hit "crawl now" at any time. The first crawl of a new competitor builds a baseline; real changes appear from the second crawl onward.

Does the AI make up the numbers?

No. Change detection is arithmetic: we compare two captures of the same product page. The AI only writes the sentence around those facts, and is told to use no figure that is not in them. If the model is unavailable you still get a correct report, just a plainer one.

Which platforms do you support?

Any storefront that publishes product pages: Shopify, WooCommerce, BigCommerce, PrestaShop, Magento and Bol.com. We read the structured product data each platform already publishes, which is more accurate and far lighter on their servers than scraping listing pages.

How many competitors do I need?

Most small shops have three to eight that genuinely compete. The free plan tracks three, which is enough to be useful; Pro covers fifteen, which is enough to be thorough.

Can I cancel?

Any time, from the billing tab. No email required and no retention call. You keep access to your data and your reports until the end of the period you have already paid for.

What happens to my data if I leave?

You can delete your workspace at any time, which removes your shops, competitors, captures and reports. Deletion is immediate.
