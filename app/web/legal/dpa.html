Data processing

Last updated 29 September 2026.

This summarises how Sparton processes personal data on your behalf. Our privacy policy covers what we do as a controller for your own account; this page covers the processor role, which applies when your organisation's data is what we are handling.

Roles

You are the controller of your account data. Sparton is the processor. We act on your documented instructions — in practice, the shop and competitor URLs you add, and the crawl schedule your plan sets.

Subject matter, duration and nature

Processing runs for as long as your account is active. The work performed is the product: fetching public product pages, extracting price and stock fields, diffing them over time, and generating alerts and reports.

Categories of data

CategoryWhat it covers
Account dataName, email, organisation, hashed password
Commercial dataShops, competitors, product names, prices, stock, captures, alerts, reports
Technical dataIP addresses, request logs, session tokens
Special categoriesNone. We do not collect or infer health, biometric, racial, political or religious data, and you must not submit it.

Sub-processors

We use a small number of suppliers, each bound by contract to process data only on our instructions:

  • Stripe — payment processing and billing records.
  • Postmark / your SMTP provider — transactional email delivery.
  • Our hosting provider — database and application hosting, within the EU.
  • Our model provider (OpenRouter) — report narrative text only. Numeric figures are computed locally and are never sent to a model.

Security measures

TLS in transit, hashed passwords, per-organisation row-level access control on every query, and signed, idempotent webhooks for payment events. Access to production data is limited to engineers who need it and is logged.

Sub-processors outside the EEA

Some suppliers process data outside the European Economic Area. Where they do, transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision. You may request a copy of the clauses we have executed.

Your assistance and audit

We will help you respond to a data-subject request, and you may audit our processing once per year. We prefer to resolve findings by correspondence and will not charge for this unless your request is manifestly unfounded.

Deletion

On termination we delete your data within 30 days, except billing records, which we must retain for tax purposes.
