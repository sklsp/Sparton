Privacy

Last updated 29 September 2026.

SPARTON watches public product pages on online shops so that you can see what your competitors are doing. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how long we keep it. It is written to be read, not to be skimmed past.

What we collect

  • Your account. Email address, a hashed password, and the name of your organisation. We never store your password in a readable form.
  • Your data. The shops and competitors you add, the products, prices and stock levels we capture from their public pages, and the alerts and reports derived from that history.
  • Usage. Which pages you use, how many model tokens each report consumed, and basic request logs containing IP addresses. We use these to keep the service working and to bill you accurately.

What we do not collect

  • We do not store the HTML of the pages we crawl. We keep the fields we extracted — name, price, availability — and the URL they came from.
  • We do not buy or sell contact lists, and we do not use your data to train models.
  • We do not track you across other websites.

Who we crawl, and what we tell them

We crawl public product pages of the shops and competitors you add. We respect robots.txt: a page that forbids us is skipped, not worked around. Every request identifies itself honestly as SPARTON with a contact address, so a site owner can see us in their logs, ask us to stop, or block us entirely.

Who we share it with

Stripe processes payments and holds your billing details; we never see or store your card number. Our email provider delivers verification and password-reset messages. Our hosting provider stores the database. None of them may use your data for their own purposes. We do not sell data to anyone.

How long we keep it

Your account data is kept while your account exists. Deleted shops, competitors and their captures are removed immediately. Reports follow your plan's retention window. We keep billing records for as long as tax law requires, and operational logs for 30 days.

Your rights

You can export or delete everything we hold about you at any time by writing to us. We respond within 30 days. If you are in the EU or UK you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction and portability; we honour all of them. You may also complain to your data protection authority, and we would rather you told us first.

Security

Passwords are hashed with a memory-hard function. Sessions are opaque random tokens. Data in transit is encrypted with TLS. Access to production is limited to the people who need it. No system is perfect, but a breach of this data would tell us that we failed, and we would tell you.
