Sparton vs Prisync

Both watch competitor prices. They are built for different shops. Here are the facts side by side; every Prisync figure links to Prisync's own pricing page, so you can check it.

Prisync facts read from prisync.com/pricing on 1 October 2026. Prices change; check the source before you decide.

Comparison

Topic Sparton Prisync
Cheapest way to start Free — 1 shop, 3 competitors, checked weekly. No card. our plans $99 per month (Professional, 100 products), after a 14-day free trial without a card. source
What a plan counts Shops and competitors. Every product a competitor sells is read. Products: 100, 1,000 or 5,000 per plan, with unlimited competitors. source
How competitors are added Sparton suggests shops that sell what you sell; you confirm the ones to watch. No URLs to paste. URL-based plans: you add competitor URLs one by one or in bulk, per product. Channel-based plans track the competitors on your sales channels, from $199 per month. source
How often prices are checked Weekly on Free, daily on Pro, twice a day on Business. Three times a day on URL-based plans; daily on channel-based plans. source
API access Included on Business. Available from Premium, charged at an extra 20% on top of the monthly fee. source
Currency Euro, prices excluding VAT. US dollars. source
How sure each price is Labelled per competitor: exact when read from the shop's own feed, "extracted" when read from the page. Not stated on the pricing page, so not compared here.

Sparton fits when…

  • you run one to a few small webshops in the Netherlands or Belgium;
  • you'd rather confirm suggested competitors than collect URLs per product;
  • a weekly report in plain language, with the evidence linked, is what you will actually read.

Prisync may fit better when…

  • you track a large catalogue against a set list of competitor product pages;
  • you need prices checked several times a day on every plan;
  • you want to track competitors across marketplaces and sales channels.
